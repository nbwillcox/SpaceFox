# SpaceFox

A free, retro-flavored on-rails space shooter with neon-meets-painted art: pilot the Firebrand through canyons and fleets, dodge and shoot, and take on the Regent's Gloom Armada.

**Play it in your browser:** https://nbwillcox.github.io/SpaceFox/

The campaign is complete (milestone 5): seven stages, a route map with a hard branch, two all-range arenas and the final boss. Polish and balance are still ongoing. See `DESIGN.md` for the full plan.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Steer | Mouse (move the pointer), `W A S D` or the arrow keys |
| Fire | Left click or `Space`: tap for a shot, **hold to charge**, sweep up to 3 lock-ons, release for a homing volley (no target: a charged blast) |
| Barrel roll | Right click, `Q` / `E`, or double-tap left / right: **deflects enemy shots back** at them while you spin: two full turns of the ship with a sideways dash and a deflect bubble |
| Boost / brake | `Shift` / `Ctrl` |
| Bomb | `B` |
| Pause | `P` or `Esc` |
| Gamepad | Left stick / d-pad steer, `A` fire, `B` bomb, `X`/`LB` roll left, `Y`/`RB` roll right, `LT` brake, `RT` boost, Start pause |

**Wingmates:** Juno and Bram sometimes fly into the rail stages with Gloom fighters on their tail. Shoot the chasers off before their shield runs out for a rescue bonus (and credits).

Pickups: laser upgrades (twin, then hyper), bombs, repair, overshield, boost refill, extra retries and stars. Settings has *Rapid fire while FIRE is held* (default on for phones) if you prefer holding to charging.

Stage 1 ends with the **Bulwark**, an armored walking fortress: plain lasers barely scratch it (lock-on volleys, deflected mortars and bombs do full damage), it telegraphs claw swipes with a red lane on the water, and once half its armor is gone the furnace core opens. Moss and Captain Vale chatter over the radio with typed text and a babble voice (Settings > Comms chatter: full / warnings only / off).

## Campaign

The title screen has a *How to play* page, and every stage has its own music style (canyon, water, foundry, space, tunnel, warp, arena, final).

Pick a difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard) and fly the **route map**: Ember Canyon, then a fork between *Drift Belt* (asteroids, easy route) and *Glasswater Reach* (hard route), then *Foundry Spine* (lava) or *Ghost Station* (tunnels). Each stage ends with a boss: Bulwark, Rockjaw, Tidecaller, Foundry Warden and Prism Sentinel. Then the all-range *Nebula Arena* (rival ace Scarlet Talon), the warp-lane *Gatewyrm Run* (fly through gold rings, then fight the serpent), the all-range *Regent's Fleet* (knock out the Hollow Queen's shield turrets, then her hull) and the final *Crown Core*, a three-phase fortress boss.

**All-range arenas:** no rails. Left/right turn the ship, up/down pitch the nose (invert in Settings), the radar in the corner shows every contact (top = straight ahead), and the edge of the arena steers you back. Lasers bend a little toward the enemy nearest your reticle.

- **Medals:** Bronze, silver or gold from the share of enemies you destroyed. Gold on a stage opens the hard route after it.
- **Credits and the hangar:** Stars, score and medals earn credits. Spend them between stages on laser core, shield cells, bomb bay, lock-on array, roll servos, boost cells and a star magnet, or restock bombs.
- **Retries and continues** depend on the difficulty. *Stage Select* on the title screen replays any stage you have unlocked.

All of these can be changed in **Settings > Key bindings**.

### On a phone or tablet

Play in landscape. A floating stick on the left steers the ship; on the right, **FIRE** (hold) shoots, the two **ROLL** buttons barrel-roll left or right to deflect shots, and **BOOST**, **BRAKE** and **BOMB** sit above them. The pause button opens the menu. The on-screen controls appear on touch devices automatically; Settings has size, opacity and a left-handed layout. Use your browser's *Add to Home Screen* to install it as a full-screen app.


The mouse is locked to the game while you fly so a stray click cannot leave the window; press `Esc` to release it (the game pauses).
