    HOW TO PLAY

    CONTROLS

    Steer mouse · W A S D / arrows · left stick

    Fire left click · Space · A. Tap to shoot, hold to charge a lock-on

    Barrel roll right click · Q / E, or double-tap left / right · LB / RB

    Boost / brake Shift / Ctrl · RT / LT

    Bomb B · B button   Pause P / Esc · Start

    On a phone: a floating stick on the left and buttons on the right. Everything can be rebound in Settings.

    TIPS

    Lock-on: hold fire, sweep the reticle over targets, release for a homing volley. Locking the same boss several times multiplies the hit.

    Deflect: a barrel roll throws enemy shots back at them.

    Wingmates: shoot the fighters off Juno's or Bram's tail before their shield runs out.

    Rings: in the warp lane, chain gold rings for points and shield.

    Bombs: clear enemy shots and hurt everything on screen. Save them for bosses.

    Medals: destroy most of the enemies for gold. Gold opens the hard route.
